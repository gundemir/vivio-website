When you use the enquiry fields

The topic, name and message fields are used in your browser to prepare an email. The website does not submit those fields to a server or save them in a website database. “Open email draft” passes the draft to your chosen email application. “Copy enquiry” copies it to your device’s clipboard. Vivio receives your message only if you send the email.

Your browser, device and chosen email application may retain information according to their own settings. The website does not use local storage to keep your draft between visits.

When you send an email

Your sender address, message and any details you choose to include reach Vivio’s business mailbox. They are used to answer your enquiry, provide a requested report, discuss a possible engagement and maintain the related correspondence. Please avoid patient information or confidential scientific material in an initial enquiry.

Email delivery and storage involve email-service providers; website delivery involves hosting and network-service providers. These services receive the information needed to perform their respective functions. Authorised Vivio personnel handle enquiries. Information may also be disclosed to competent authorities where legally required.

Purpose and legal basis

For enquiries connected with a possible engagement, the relevant KVKK ground is processing necessary to establish or perform a contract, Article 5(2)(c), where its conditions are met. Responding to general professional enquiries and protecting the service may rely on legitimate interests under Article 5(2)(f), subject to the required balance with your rights. Records required by law are handled under the applicable legal-obligation ground. Sending an enquiry is not treated as blanket consent to marketing.

If the EU or UK GDPR applies to a particular processing activity, the corresponding bases may include pre-contractual steps under Article 6(1)(b), legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f), or a legal obligation under Article 6(1)(c), as applicable.

Website requests, cookies and storage

Serving a web page involves technical request data, such as an IP address, requested address and browser information. Infrastructure providers may keep operational or security logs. The website code contains no analytics service, advertising tracker or third-party font service, and does not set cookies or write to local storage. Hosting infrastructure and your browser operate separately from that code.

Location and retention

Email and infrastructure services can involve processing outside Türkiye. The applicable arrangements depend on the providers and account configuration. For information about service providers, processing locations and the arrangements relevant to your enquiry, contact Vivio.

The website creates no stored enquiry record. For correspondence received by email, retention depends on the enquiry, any resulting engagement and applicable record-keeping obligations. You may ask about or request deletion of your correspondence using the contact address above.

Your rights and requests

Article 11 of the KVKK provides rights to learn whether data is processed, obtain information about its use and recipients, seek correction, and request deletion or destruction where the legal conditions apply. You may request notification of corrections or deletion to recipients, object to an adverse result produced solely by automated analysis and seek compensation for unlawful processing.

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, the relevant rights may also include access, restriction, objection and portability. Send privacy requests to gundemir@vivioresearch.com. You can also use the complaint routes available through the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or the competent authority for an applicable jurisdiction.
