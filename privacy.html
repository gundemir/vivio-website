4. Legal bases for processing

Under the GDPR, we rely on the performance of steps taken at your request prior to a contract (Article 6(1)(b)), our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and keeping our site secure (Article 6(1)(f)), and your consent where you voluntarily choose to contact us (Article 6(1)(a)).

Under the KVKK, we rely on the grounds set out in Article 5, in particular processing that is necessary for the establishment or performance of a contract (Article 5/2-c) and processing necessary for our legitimate interests (Article 5/2-f), and, where applicable, your explicit consent (Article 5/1).

We keep enquiry data only for as long as needed to respond to you and for a reasonable period afterwards, after which we delete or anonymise it, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

If the GDPR applies to you, you also have the rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, and data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at gundemir@vivioresearch.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu, kvkk.gov.tr) or, if you are in the EEA or the UK, with your local data protection supervisory authority.
